A cost-reduction firm that needed to look modern and bold, without losing its mark.

P3 Cost Analysts audits what businesses pay their vendors, from telecom and utilities to waste and merchant processing, and delivers savings with no upfront cost.

Their old logo, three mountain triangles with a P3 inside, had equity worth keeping. I rebuilt it as a modern, bold mark, set the identity around it, and designed the website that is now live.

The idea: three triangles become three cube faces, with the P3 cut in

Where it started.

The old logo: three mountain triangles with a P3 standing in front. Recognisable, but soft and dated.

Three triangles, three faces.

The three triangles became the three faces of a cube: Purple Haze on top, Deep Cove and Tealish Blue on the sides. Solid where the old mark was soft.

P3, cut in.

The P and the 3 are cut into the cube in light tones, so the name is part of the form, not placed on top of it.

Same DNA. Modern and bold.

One mark that leads the stacked primary logo, the horizontal lockup and the app-sized icon.

The identity system

A logo suite for light and dark surfaces, a palette led by deep blue-violet gradients with a warm sunset accent, and a two-family type system.
