A brand-new AI that answers the phone for aesthetic practices, and needed to sound human doing it.

Kaia is one system for aesthetic and plastic surgery practices: an AI receptionist that answers every call 24/7 and books consults in real time, a CRM that follows up every lead, and an EMR built for aesthetic charting. New business, no identity, and a launch date at ASPS in Houston.

[1] Kaia AI[2] Kaia CRM[3] Kaia EMR

  • Make it a voice

    Kaia isn’t a chatbot. It’s a receptionist you talk to, so the brand had to carry sound.

  • Put the K first

    The client wanted the letter K to be the hero of the mark, readable at any size.

  • Read as AI, and as trustworthy

    Clinicians had to see a capable AI tool, not a sci-fi gimmick.

TeamMe, soloTime3 weeksDeadlineASPS · Oct 15–18

The idea: one mark, three answers

A voice that carries.

The left half radiates outward like a voice travelling down the line: a call going out, answered. Its four strokes also echo the cross practices already trust.

A K you can’t miss.

The right half curves like a sound wave and closes the letterform, so the K reads first on a favicon, a phone screen or a booth banner.

One spark of AI.

A single cerulean spark, the only accent in the mark, signals intelligence without robot clichés, and became the brand’s highlight colour everywhere.

Voice, K, AI. One mark.

Set beside a calm wordmark, the icon works as the primary logo, a stacked secondary and a stand-alone app icon.

The identity system

A logo suite for every surface, a colour palette led by Green Vogue and the cerulean spark, and a three-family type system.
